The GPU checks use offscreen readback. The separate Flutter tests switch through
slice, isosurface, vector, streamline, temporal and volume views and require zero
readback bytes for presented frames. They exercise temporal seeking, picking,
authorized agent changes, retry behavior and desktop/narrow layouts. The shared
timeline releases frame demand on pause and engine disposal.

Native resource accounting reports zero owned bytes, live shader programs and
materials after volume teardown. This does not measure physical GPU residency.
Volume compositing uses opaque scene depth. It does not establish correct ordering
against transparent surfaces or overlapping volumes.

The macOS lab was also inspected through its accessibility tree and screenshots.

Automated checks are distinct from a human-operated acceptance test. Flutter
logged AXTree update warnings during this inspection; comprehensive accessibility
