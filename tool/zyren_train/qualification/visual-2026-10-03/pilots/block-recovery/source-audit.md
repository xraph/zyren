## What the permitted history identifies

The teacher uses own world pose, a private waypoint cursor and live target pose in `examples/game_lab/training_worker/lib/visual_scenario.dart:174`. Its cursor starts at zero (line 80), advances inside a 0.35 m radius (line 182), and then selects the live target after the two authored waypoints. The actor receives captured A6 camera planes and eight own-body features. `packages/zyren_game_ai/lib/src/brain/training_visual_profiles.dart:103` supplies local velocity, angularVelocityY, height, fixed goal [1,0] and validity; line 135 rejects other goal values. It supplies no private cursor, current target pose or world XZ position.

The controller produces world-space movement intent in `packages/zyren_game_native/lib/src/character.dart:223`. The shared motor rotates the capsule and camera toward that movement in `packages/zyren_characters/lib/physics.dart:90`. A single camera/body frame can therefore alias different goals or route histories. The fixed goal fields do not solve route intent.

The native TRAIN audit found one byte-identical current-frame pair with different movement labels: seeds 11 and 13 at proposal 327, route phase 2. Their last eight input frames were also identical, while their 32-frame, 64-frame and complete histories differed. Initial camera cues had distinct target centroids, 36.5 versus 26.09 columns in the diagnostic depth ROI. This requires retaining a permitted earlier cue; it does not justify reading a hidden current target. A near route-phase pair within seed 7 had camera RMS difference 0.0000415 and opposite lateral labels, but its recent histories were distinct. The bounded nearest search uses coarse native-image pooling followed by exact raw-input comparison. Similarity is not a proof of impossible inference.

Across all 4,200 frames, no identical complete input prefixes had conflicting labels. At seed 1004's first mistake, the independently replayed teacher and student saw exactly the same input history and teacher label, while the learned actor chose the opposite side. That is a model error. At the other first divergences, permitted local velocity and angularY integrated from the known authored reset pose reconstructed own XZ within 0.000019 m. There is useful route-progress information in the history. Naive integration drifted by as much as 1.75 m later in a full course, so it is not a qualified localization implementation. These finite audits do not establish observability for every locked stress case, particularly a moving target after occlusion.

## Proposed next mechanism, not implemented

More low-rate motor cloning has not solved the route transitions. A more reliable candidate would factor learned visual goal estimation from the existing public navigation and character controller. A trained CNN/recurrent estimator would emit a visible target bearing/range, validity and uncertainty from actual camera pixels. A host service would retain that permitted observation with its capture tick, captured own pose, actor generation and episode. Existing belief/provenance rules would govern expiry and any motion extrapolation. The service must never substitute current hidden target geometry.

The existing `NavigationFollower` and character navigation branch (`packages/zyren_game_native/lib/src/character.dart:203`) can control own movement toward that remembered goal using an explicitly admitted authored navigation map and permitted obstacle updates. They should compute their own route rather than import the teacher's private waypoint cursor. Own pose is legitimate self-state; hidden target pose is not. An optional learned low-level head could consume the resulting public local navigation intent, but that would need its own qualification.

This is an explicit new visual-navigation-v2 contract proposal. It needs a new observation/model/profile hash, declared learned outputs, planner/config/source pins, goal-provenance rules, a revised native worker/runtime binding and an immutable evaluation plan locked before training. Preserve every failed v1 plan and receipt. Keep success, contact, split and paired-hidden-world gates at least as strict. Test camera dependence by changing or removing the initial permitted cue, not merely blanking frames after a valid goal was already remembered. Native parity must compare learned outputs and final typed controller continuation for the whole deployed pipeline. A scripted route plus a decorative CNN would not satisfy camera-dependent behavior. No new training budget or implementation is claimed here.
